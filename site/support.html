Common questions

How do I invite friends?

Open your plan and tap Invite. Share the link in your group chat. Anyone with the link can join the plan, and it works for the whole group.

My friend doesn't have the app. Can they still join?

Yes. When they open your link they'll be asked to get Pull Up (it's free). After installing, they go back to the link and tap Open in Pull Up to land right in the plan.

How does picking a spot work?

Everyone in the plan swipes through places nearby. Once enough of the group likes the same place, it locks in automatically. The host can also just pick a spot.

Who can see my location?

Only people in the same plan, and only while you're sharing for that plan. Sharing starts when you tap Share ETA and stops when you tap Stop, when the plan ends, or after 6 hours. Choose ETA only if you'd rather your crew see just how many minutes away you are, not where you are.

Does Pull Up track me in the background?

No. Pull Up only uses your location while you're sharing your ETA for a plan. On newer versions, sharing keeps working if you switch to a maps app, and iPhone shows the blue location indicator the whole time. When you're not sharing, Pull Up doesn't use your location in the background.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Settings → Notifications → Pull Up on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Profile tab, scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, plans you created, your messages and your location history.
