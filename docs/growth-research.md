# Growth research: what drives consumer adoption (Oct 2026)

Research pass on the group-plans / friend-location category. Sourced facts are
linked; company-reported or blog figures may be inflated. Items marked
*judgment* are opinion.

**Bottom line:** every Pull Up feature exists elsewhere. The thing that predicts
winning here is **how little the invited guests have to do**. Top move: guests
vote, see the plan, and get directions from a web link with **no install**.
Then pick a narrow wedge (one campus/scene) and build a weekly habit.

## Landscape

### What worked
- **Partiful.** Guests RSVP without installing or signing up
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partiful)), so every invite is
  marketing. Reported ~500K MAU (Q1 2025), 400% YoY, 60% of active users check
  weekly ([NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/partiful-marketing-strategy/)).
  Distinctive aesthetic, IRL stunts, frat/club seeding, free for ~5 years.
  Survived Apple Invites (Feb 2025)
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/04/apple-launches-invites-a-new-app-for-creating-custom-invitations)).
- **Posh.** A nightlife discovery feed; 8M users, $350M GMV, $37M Series B, Mar 2026
  ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/19/exclusive-posh-lands-37m-series-b-what-are-we-doing-tonight-problem/)).
  Earns from ticket transactions, not by charging people to use the app.
- **Howbout.** A shared calendar; 4M users, mostly ≤25; 75%+ share their calendar
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/13/howbout-raises-8m-from-goodwater-to-build-a-calendar-that-you-can-share-with-your-friends)).
- **Timeleft / 222 / Pie.** Meet strangers, which sidesteps "your friends must
  join." Timeleft runs a fixed weekly Wednesday dinner ritual.
- **Life360.** 95.8M MAU in Q4 2025
  ([IR](https://investors.life360.com/news-releases/news-release-details/life360-reports-record-q4-2025-results)).
  Location sold on safety, which people will pay for.
- **Find My.** ~70% of Gen Z use it; 40% share their location with 3+ people
  ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/07/17/many-gen-z-use-find-my-and-other-apps-to-share-their-location-with-friend-groups-24-7/)).
  Friend location is already free and always on.

### What failed
- **IRL.** ~95% of its 20M users were bots; shut down 2023, founder charged
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/irl-shut-down-fake-users/)).
  Paid installs don't build a real friend network.
- **Zenly.** 40M MAU when Snap killed it to cut costs
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/01/zenly-is-still-hugely-popular-so-whys-snap-shutting-it-down/)).
  Demand for playful friend location is real, and that gap is still open.
- **Geneva.** Bought by Bumble, no revenue, folded into Bumble BFF in 2025
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building)).
- **Google Maps Group Planning** (2018). Friends voting on a place shortlist
  never became a habit
  ([Google](https://blog.google/products/maps/all-together-now-group-planning-google-maps/)).
- **"Tinder for restaurants" clones.** Where2Eat, Unforkd, DinnerWhere, Foodie
  (2015)… *Judgment:* swiping alone isn't a differentiator.
- **2025–26 newcomers** (Clyx, Washed Up, Mmotion) all launch city by city
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)).

## Adoption barriers
1. **Every guest must download.** A plan is only as good as its least engaged
   invitee. Win the organizer, the "hard side"
   ([Andrew Chen](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/atomic-network)).
2. **The group chat already exists.** iMessage/WhatsApp handle most of "where
   should we go?" Live inside the chat; don't try to replace it (*judgment*).
3. **Location privacy.** The Instagram Map backlash in Aug 2025
   ([Spectrum](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/news/2025/08/11/what-to-know-about-instagram-s-new-location-sharing-map-feature-as-bipartisan-duo-of-senators-blast-it)).
   Find My's open-ended sharing causes awkwardness, which leaves an opening for
   location that turns off when the plan ends.
4. **Decision effort.** 5 people × 20 swipes is more work than one person
   deciding. Host mode is the right instinct.
5. **Frequency.** 2–6 plans a month is easy to forget. Fix it with a ritual
   (Timeleft) or a habit of following other people's plans (Partiful).

## Recommendations (in priority order)
1. **Guests join with no install (web page; App Clip optional).** Vote, see the
   venue/time, Open in Maps. Only the host and anyone who wants ETAs need the
   app. Covers Android too. ~1–3 weeks. *Metric:* % of invitees who vote, and
   % of guests who later host.
2. **Rich link previews and posting results back to the group chat.** Previews
   show vibe, time and "3/5 voted"; one tap shares "📍 Locked: X, 8pm." Make
   the in-app chat secondary. Days of work.
3. **Lead with the night itself.** *"Pick the spot in 30 seconds. See who's on
   the way. Location turns off when the night ends."* Copy, screenshots, ASO.
4. **Start narrow.** One campus or scene. Recruit 3–5 people who always
   organize; aim for 20 active crews. Measure weekly active crews, not installs.
   Never buy incentivized installs.
5. **Habit loops.**
   - Saved crews (one-tap "the usual")
   - A weekly ritual nudge ("Thursday drinks?")
   - A shareable recap card after each plan
6. **Content.** TikTok/Reels of "nobody can decide" → a 30-second lock. Run one
   IRL stunt in your launch scene.
7. **Don't charge yet.** Later: labeled promoted venues, reservation affiliate
   links, host themes, org accounts. Never paywall guests.

**Skip for now:** a social/discovery feed, a full iMessage app, and native
Android before the guest web page exists.

**North-star metric:** % of invited guests who take part without installing →
% who later host.
